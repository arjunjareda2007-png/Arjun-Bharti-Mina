Biography & Background

Arjun Bharti Mina (widely known as ABM) is an independent Indian rapper, singer-songwriter, lyricist, composer, author, and creative technologist from Rajasthan and Jaipur. Blending underground Desi Hip-Hop with Indian melodic cadence, Arjun writes, composes, and produces original music while graduating in Bachelor of Technology (B.Tech) in Civil Engineering from Swami Keshvanand Institute of Technology, Management & Gramothan (SKIT), Jaipur (2022–2026).
